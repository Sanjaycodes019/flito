downloaded unchanged on 5 October 2026. The logos are trademarks of their owners
and are shown only to identify the account a shipper is paying into. Check with
each bank (and eSewa's and Khalti's merchant brand guidelines) before a public
launch, and replace a file here when a bank changes its branding.
